# Store listing copy (draft)

Rules for all copy, ads and screenshots: show only clothed results; never use any of the wording banned in SPEC.md ("Safety guardrails"), or anything suggesting clothing can be taken away or seen through; describe the feature as placing fabric over the garment area. Screenshots must show the real app.

## Default listing: Women

- **Name:** Snipfit: Try Fabric On You
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30 chars):** See colors on you before buying
- **Short description (Play, 80 chars):** Hold your photo over any fabric in the store and see the color on you.
- **Description:**

  See any fabric on you before you buy.

  Take a selfie and Snipfit cuts out your top, right on your phone. In the store, point your camera at a shirt or a dress and the real fabric shows through your photo, so you can see the color next to your face before you buy.

  - Works in seconds, no account needed
  - Your photos stay on your phone
  - Drag, resize and lock your photo over any fabric; torch and exposure lock for dim stores
  - Snap up to four options and compare them side by side with color codes
  - Share a reveal video or a "Which one?" collage to Stories, TikTok, Snapchat or WhatsApp

  Snipfit Pro: AI try-on shows the full look and drape of a garment on you (30 a month), plus shares without a watermark. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; cancel anytime in your account settings.

- **Screenshots (6.9" and 6.5" iPhone, Android phone):** 1) cutout over a patterned top in a store, "See any fabric on you"; 2) compare grid with four colors and swatches, "Compare before you buy"; 3) refine screen, "Perfect your cutout"; 4) share sheet with the reveal video, "Share in two taps"; 5) privacy screen, "Your photos stay on your phone"; 6) AI look side by side with true color (clothed, labeled AI-generated), "See the full look with Pro".
