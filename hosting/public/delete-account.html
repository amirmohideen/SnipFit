Delete your Snipfit account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Snipfit and tap the Settings icon.
  2. Under Privacy, tap Delete account and confirm.

This immediately deletes your account, remaining AI credits, consent records, any AI try-on photos or results on our servers (these are deleted within 24 hours anyway), and the photos stored in the app on that phone.

Without the app

Email privacy@snipfit.app from any address with the subject "Delete my Snipfit account". Include your order number from Apple or Google if you bought Pro or credits, so we can find your account. We will confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: account ID and sign-in link, credit balance and ledger, Pro status record, consent records, push tokens, AI inputs and results, request records.
  • Kept: minimal purchase records we must keep for tax and accounting, for the period the law requires. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account doesn't cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
